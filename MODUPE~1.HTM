Ranked #9 in the Q3 2026 edition of Top 10 Executive Coaches — Global. Assessed against the published methodology.

Dr Modupe Taylor-Pearce

Sierra Leone

Co-founder and CEO, BCA Leadership; founding Dean, African Leadership University School of Business

Co-founder and CEO of BCA Leadership, a pan-African CEO membership and leadership development organisation. West Point (BSc), Cornell (MEng) and Capella (PhD in Leadership, Organization and Management). Founding Dean of the African Leadership University School of Business.

Why this placement

His documentation quality is among the cleanest on the shortlist — every recognition is verifiable on the awarding body's own site rather than only in his own materials: Thinkers50 Coaching Legend (2025), Coaches50 (2024), Thinkers50 Radar Class of 2022, and the 2021 coaching award shortlist.

He is also building C-suite coaching capacity in a market the recognition bodies barely reach, which is a contribution the Anglo-American circuit consistently under-counts. He ranks ninth because, under the interlink adjustment, that recognition is one signal rather than four, and his published work does not yet carry independent weight.

Editorial notes and exclusions

  • Most of his books are self-published through Lulu, including Business Bomba (2022).
  • Organisational scale claims — 2,000 African leaders, largest such organisation in Africa — are unaudited and attributed rather than asserted. BCA Leadership is registered in Mauritius; that is not his personal location.
