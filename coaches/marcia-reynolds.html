Ranked #6 in the Q3 2026 edition of Top 10 Executive Coaches — Global. Assessed against the published methodology.

Dr Marcia Reynolds

Arizona, USA

President, Covisioning LLC; Master Certified Coach

President of Covisioning LLC and a Master Certified Coach, with a doctorate in organizational psychology and master's degrees in communication and adult learning.

Why this placement

Five books with Berrett-Koehler — Breakthrough Coaching (2024), Coach the Person, Not the Problem (2020), The Discomfort Zone (2014) and Wander Woman (2010) — represent a sustained relationship with a serious independent publisher, and Coach the Person, Not the Problem is used as training material by other coaches rather than only read by clients.

ICF's own site records that she was the fifth global president of the International Coaching Federation and among the first twenty-five people worldwide to hold the MCC credential. She holds the ICF Circle of Distinction.

Editorial notes and exclusions

  • One correction: the 2024 ICF Coaching Impact Award was conferred on her client organisation AEON Vietnam in the Emerging Organizations category, not on her personally, and is recorded here accordingly.
  • Her ICF honours carry governance adjacency, since she formerly led the body. A "Neuroplastician" credential is a purchased commercial certification, not an award, and is excluded. The year of her Circle of Distinction could not be located.
