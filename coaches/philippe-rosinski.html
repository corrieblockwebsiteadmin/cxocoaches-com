Philippe Rosinski

Rhode-Saint-Genèse, Belgium

Principal, Rosinski & Company; creator of the Cultural Orientations Framework

Principal of Rosinski & Company since 1999. MSc in Electrical Engineering from Stanford and an Executive Master in Management from Solvay Brussels School. Creator of the Cultural Orientations Framework.

Why this placement

The ICF Circle of Distinction (2022) is verified on ICF's own past-winners page — an independent, dated credential owing nothing to the Thinkers50 network, which on this methodology is worth more than several better-publicised honours.

Coaching Across Cultures (Nicholas Brealey, 2003) and Global Coaching (Nicholas Brealey, 2010) opened cross-cultural coaching as a distinct area of practice and have been translated into six languages. The Cultural Orientations Framework is a published instrument other practitioners use. He ranks seventh because recent output is thin relative to those above him.

Editorial notes and exclusions

  • Guest teaching at Cambridge and Harvard is not a faculty appointment and is not rendered here as an affiliation.
  • A 2003 Harvard Business School "featured book recommendation" is book-promotion copy, not an institutional honour. "First European to be designated MCC" is unverifiable. A "World's Top 30 Coaching Professionals" claim names no awarding body.
