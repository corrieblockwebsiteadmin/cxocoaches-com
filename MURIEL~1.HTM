Ranked #4 in the Q3 2026 edition of Top 10 Executive Coaches — Global. Assessed against the published methodology.

Muriel Wilkins

Washington, DC, USA

CEO and founder, Paravis Partners; host, Coaching Real Leaders (Harvard Business Review)

CEO and founder of Paravis Partners, a leadership advisory firm. Previously on the senior executive team of U.S. News & World Report with P&L responsibility, and earlier at Accenture and The Prudential. MBA, Harvard Business School.

Why this placement

Coaching Real Leaders, her Harvard Business Review podcast, is in its tenth season and consists of recorded coaching sessions with real executives, debriefed afterwards. Measured against the six thought-leadership markers this is close to the strongest body of public teaching in the profession: specific, because it is actual practice rather than description of practice; transferable, because the method is visible move by move; and intellectually honest in a way almost no coaching content manages, because the sessions include the moments that do not land. Most coaches write about coaching. She shows it.

Leadership Unblocked (Harvard Business Review Press, 2025) and Own the Room (HBR Press, 2013, with Amy Jen Su) are both on a serious publisher. Shortlisted for the Thinkers50 Coaching & Mentoring Award 2025.

Editorial notes and exclusions

  • She holds no governance role at any body that has recognised her — the cleanest file on this list.
  • Reported as shortlisted, not a recipient; the 2025 award went to Hull and Moore. An "award-winning" podcast label and a USA Today bestseller placement could not be verified and are excluded.
